Village Roots Initiative

Helping youth connect, create and belong.

Village Roots is a community-based resource hub where families discover local programs, events, mentors, organizations, and services that help young people connect in person — all in one trusted, accessible place.

How it works

From search to showing up, in three steps

Village Roots does the gathering and vetting, so families can spend less time searching and more time doing.

1

Discover

Browse local programs, events, mentors, and services by category, age group, and neighborhood — from weekly classes to one-day workshops.

2

Connect

Reach out directly to verified providers. Every listing shows what to expect, who it's for, and how to sign up — no guesswork, no gatekeeping.

3

Grow

Show up in person. Skills, confidence, and friendships take root when young people make things, move together, and give back — face to face.

About Village Roots

It takes a village. We're the map of it.

Village Roots Initiative started with a simple observation: our community is full of people and programs doing wonderful things for young people — but families often can't find them, and providers often can't reach the families who need them most.

So we built the bridge. Village Roots gathers local youth-building opportunities — arts, athletics, mindfulness, outdoor education, life skills, and service — into one trusted, accessible hub. We believe young people thrive when they connect in person, create with their hands, and feel they belong somewhere real.

Questions

Good to know

For families

Parents, guardians, and caregivers

Does Village Roots cost anything?
No. Browsing and connecting through Village Roots is completely free for families. Individual programs set their own fees, and we highlight free and low-cost options wherever we can.
What ages do you serve?
Most listings serve youth from elementary through high school. Every listing states its age range, so you can filter to what fits your family.
How do I know a program is safe?
Every listing is reviewed by our team before it goes live, and we confirm the organization is real, active, and youth-appropriate. We still encourage families to ask providers their own questions — good programs welcome them.
How do I sign my child up?
Sign-ups happen directly with each provider. Every listing includes contact details and enrollment instructions, so you're always one step from the source.

For providers

Programs, mentors, and organizations

Who can list on Village Roots?
Any local program, organization, mentor, or service that helps young people connect, create, and grow in person — from established nonprofits to a neighbor teaching woodworking.
Is listing free?
Yes. Listing is free. Our goal is a complete picture of what's available to local youth, not a pay-to-play directory.
What does verification involve?
You submit your details through our form, and our team confirms your organization is real and active and that your offering is youth-appropriate. Most reviews are completed within a week.
Can I update my listing later?
Yes. You can update schedules, capacity, and details anytime, and we periodically re-check listings to keep the hub current.
